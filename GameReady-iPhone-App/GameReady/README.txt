Game Ready - iPhone app (no Mac needed)

BUILD THE .ipa ONLINE (free, GitHub)
1. Make a free account at github.com and create a new repository.
2. Upload everything in this folder (including the hidden .github folder) to the repository.
3. Open the Actions tab > "Build iPhone app" > Run workflow. Wait about 5 minutes.
4. Open the finished run and download the "GameReady-ipa" artifact. Unzip it to get GameReady.ipa.

INSTALL ON IPHONE
1. Install Sideloadly (sideloadly.io) on a Windows PC or Mac.
2. Connect the iPhone by cable, tap Trust, and turn on Settings > Privacy & Security > Developer Mode.
3. Drag GameReady.ipa into Sideloadly, enter your Apple ID, click Start.
4. On the phone: Settings > General > VPN & Device Management > your Apple ID > Trust.
Free Apple IDs expire the app after 7 days: just install again.

Mac with Xcode instead? Open GameReady.xcodeproj, pick your Team, press Run.
